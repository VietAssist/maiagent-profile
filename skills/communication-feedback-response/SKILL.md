# SKILL: Responding to client feedback

## When to use this skill
When the user receives feedback — positive or negative — from a client and needs to respond professionally.

## For negative or critical feedback

### Structure
1. **Acknowledge** the feedback genuinely — don't be defensive
2. **Thank them** for raising it (even if it stings)
3. **Clarify** if there is a genuine misunderstanding — calmly, not argumentatively
4. **State what you will do differently** or how you will address it
5. **Reaffirm commitment** to the relationship or project

### Tone rules
- Never argue back or list reasons why the client is wrong
- Never over-apologise to the point of sounding incompetent
- Stay warm but professional — emotion belongs in a call, not an email

## For positive feedback
- Respond briefly and genuinely — don't be dismissive ("oh it was nothing") or over-the-top
- Use it as an opportunity to reinforce the relationship
- Optional: mention what you are working on next

## Common Vietnamese writer mistakes to fix
- Accepting all blame even when not warranted
- Going silent when receiving critical feedback (no response is worse than a bad response)
- Responding emotionally or too formally

## Output format
Write the complete response. Note if a call would be more appropriate for complex or emotionally charged feedback.
