# SKILL: Introducing yourself professionally

## When to use this skill
When the user needs to write a professional self-introduction — in an email, LinkedIn message, a new team Slack, or at the start of a call.

## Written self-introduction structure (email or message)
1. **Who you are**: name, role, and one-line context for why you're reaching out
2. **What you bring**: one or two specific strengths or experiences relevant to this person or situation
3. **Why you're connecting**: be direct — what do you want from this interaction?
4. **Easy next step**: propose something specific and low-commitment ("a 15-minute call" or "happy to share my portfolio")

## Verbal introduction (for calls — give talking points)
- Name and current role
- One sentence on your background most relevant to this conversation
- One sentence on what you're hoping to get from the call
- Hand it back: "I'd love to hear more about [their work/company]"

## Tone rules
- Confident but not arrogant
- Specific — avoid generic phrases like "I am a hard-working professional"
- Warm and human — Western professionals respond to personality, not just credentials

## Common Vietnamese writer mistakes to fix
- Being too modest and underselling themselves
- Starting with education credentials before anything else
- Writing a biography instead of a focused introduction

## Output format
Write the complete introduction. Ask the user for context: who are they introducing themselves to, and what outcome do they want?
