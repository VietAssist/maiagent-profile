# SKILL: Time zone and async work etiquette

## When to use this skill
When the user is working with Western clients or teams across significant time zone differences and wants to communicate professionally and reliably across the gap.

## Core principle
Async work is not slower work — it is disciplined communication that respects everyone's time. Done well, it makes you stand out as highly professional.

## Key async etiquette rules

### Response time expectations
- Agree on response time expectations upfront with each client
- Standard async expectation: respond within 24 hours on working days
- If a message arrives outside your hours, respond at the start of your next working day — not at midnight
- If something is urgent and genuinely cannot wait, say so explicitly: "This is time-sensitive — could you respond by [time] your timezone?"

### Sending messages across time zones
- Do not expect an immediate response if you are sending at midnight their time
- Use scheduled send for non-urgent messages so they arrive at the start of their working day
- State your timezone when scheduling: "I'll send this by 5pm Vietnam time (10am London)"

### Making async updates clear
When updating across time zones, include:
- What you did / what the status is
- What you need from them (if anything)
- Your deadline or next step
- When you will next be available

### Overlap hours
- Identify the 1–3 hours of overlap between your timezone and the client's
- Protect these hours for live communication, calls, and urgent messages
- Let the client know your available overlap hours upfront

### Communicating availability clearly
"My working hours are 9am–6pm Vietnam time (GMT+7). I aim to respond to all messages within 24 hours. For urgent matters, please mark your message [URGENT]."

## Common mistakes to fix
- Responding to every message immediately at odd hours (sets unsustainable expectations)
- Going silent for 48+ hours without explanation
- Not stating your timezone — causes confusion about deadlines
- Expecting real-time responses from people 10 time zones away

## Output format
Help the user write their availability statement or async update message. Ask what the specific situation is.
