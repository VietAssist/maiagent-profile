# SKILL: Preparing talking points for video calls

## When to use this skill
When the user has an upcoming call with a Western client, manager, or interviewer and wants to feel prepared and confident about what to say.

## How to prepare

### Before the call — ask the user
1. What is the purpose of the call?
2. Who is attending from the other side?
3. What outcome do you want from this call?
4. What are you nervous about?

### Produce a call prep sheet with
1. **Opening line**: how to start the call naturally (not "Uh... so, yes, hello...")
   Example: "Thanks for making the time — I wanted to walk you through [topic] and get your thoughts."

2. **Your key points** (3–5 max): the things you must communicate, in order of importance

3. **Questions to ask them**: 2–3 prepared questions — shows engagement and curiosity

4. **Phrases for common situations**:
   - Buying time: "That's a great question — let me think about that for a second."
   - Clarifying: "Just to make sure I understand — are you saying...?"
   - Wrapping up: "Before we close, I wanted to make sure we aligned on next steps."

5. **What to do if you don't understand something**: "I want to make sure I get this right — could you say that once more?"

## Output format
Produce a one-page call prep sheet the user can keep open on a second screen during the call.
