# SKILL: Explaining complex ideas simply in English

## When to use this skill
When the user needs to explain a technical, complex, or nuanced idea to a Western client, manager, or stakeholder who may not have the same background.

## Core principle
If they have to ask what you mean, the explanation failed. Clarity is kindness.

## Simplification techniques
- **Analogy**: connect the complex idea to something familiar
- **Before/after**: show what exists now vs what you are proposing
- **The 5-year-old test**: could a non-expert understand this in one reading?
- **One sentence first**: lead with the conclusion, then explain
- **Remove jargon**: replace technical terms with plain language, or define them immediately

## Structure for explaining complex ideas
1. The simple version (1–2 sentences)
2. Why it matters to the reader (what is the impact on them?)
3. How it works (brief, in plain language)
4. What you need from them (if anything)

## Output format
Rewrite the user's explanation in plain, natural English. Ask who the audience is and what level of technical knowledge they have.
