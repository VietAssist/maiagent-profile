# SKILL: Client onboarding messages

## When to use this skill
When the user has signed a new client and needs to send the first official communications to kick off the project professionally.

## Onboarding message sequence

### 1. Welcome email (day of signing)
- Express genuine enthusiasm
- Confirm what was agreed (project, scope, timeline, first milestone)
- Introduce any tools or platforms you will use together
- State the immediate next step and who owns it

### 2. Kickoff preparation message (1–2 days before kickoff call)
- Confirm the call time and link
- Share a brief agenda
- Ask them to prepare any materials or answers you will need

### 3. Post-kickoff summary (same day or next day)
- Thank them for the call
- Summarise decisions and agreements
- List action items with owners and dates
- Confirm next milestone

## Tone for onboarding communications
- Warm and confident — they made a good decision choosing you
- Organised — show them immediately that you are professional and reliable
- Clear — no ambiguity about what happens next

## What good onboarding achieves
- Sets clear expectations on both sides
- Reduces back-and-forth and confusion
- Makes the client feel they are in good hands from day one

## Output format
Write the specific message the user needs. Ask: which stage of onboarding are they at?
