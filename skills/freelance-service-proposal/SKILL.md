# SKILL: Writing service proposals for freelance clients

## When to use this skill
When the user needs to write a proposal to win a freelance project from a Western client.

## Core principle
A proposal is not a list of services — it is a demonstration that you understand the client's problem and have a clear plan to solve it.

## Proposal structure
1. **Understanding of the problem**: show you listened — restate their situation in your own words
2. **Your proposed solution**: what you will do, specifically
3. **Scope**: exactly what is included — and what is not
4. **Deliverables**: what the client will receive and in what format
5. **Timeline**: milestones and final delivery date
6. **Investment** (price): present as an investment, not a cost — be clear and confident
7. **About you**: 2–3 sentences, relevant credentials or past results
8. **Next step**: one clear action (sign, reply to confirm, schedule a call)

## Pricing presentation
- State the price clearly — do not hide it or bury it
- Break it into phases or milestones if it's a large project
- Do not apologise for your price

## Common Vietnamese freelancer mistakes to fix
- Sending a price list instead of a tailored proposal
- Not clearly defining scope (leads to scope creep)
- Undercharging and then resenting the project
- No clear next step — proposal just ends

## Output format
Write the complete proposal. Ask the user for: client context, project scope, and their price. Format it professionally, ready to send.
