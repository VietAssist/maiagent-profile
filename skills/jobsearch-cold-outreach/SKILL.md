# SKILL: Cold outreach to Western companies or recruiters

## When to use this skill
When the user wants to reach out to a recruiter, hiring manager, or company they have no existing connection with — via LinkedIn, email, or other channels.

## Core principle
Cold outreach works when it is specific, brief, and offers something of value or shows genuine interest. Generic messages get ignored.

## LinkedIn connection request (300 character limit)
Formula: [Who you are] + [Why specifically them] + [What you want]
Example: "Hi [Name] — I've been following [Company]'s work on [specific thing]. I'm a [role] with [X years] in [field] looking for remote roles. Would love to connect."

## Cold email structure
1. **Subject**: specific and intriguing — not "Job opportunity" or "Hi"
2. **Opening**: one sentence on why you're reaching out to THIS person specifically
3. **Who you are**: 2 sentences — role, key achievement, relevance to them
4. **The ask**: one small, specific, low-commitment request (a 15-minute call, feedback on your portfolio)
5. **Close**: thank them for their time, no pressure

## Rules
- Research before writing — mention something specific about their company or work
- Keep it under 150 words for LinkedIn, under 200 for email
- One ask only — don't combine multiple requests
- Follow up once after 5–7 days if no response, then let it go

## Common Vietnamese mistakes to fix
- Sending the same generic message to 50 people
- Starting with a long story about themselves before getting to the point
- Asking for a job directly in the first message (too forward too soon)

## Output format
Write the complete message. Ask the user: who are they reaching out to and what outcome do they want?
