# SKILL: Cover letter writing

## When to use this skill
When the user needs to write a cover letter for a job application to a Western company.

## Core principle
Western hiring managers spend 30 seconds on a cover letter. It must answer: why this role, why you, and why now — fast.

## Structure
1. **Opening hook** (1–2 sentences): a specific reason you want THIS role at THIS company — not generic enthusiasm
2. **Why you** (1 paragraph): your most relevant achievement or experience. One specific example beats a list of adjectives.
3. **Why them** (1 paragraph): show you've done research — reference something specific about the company, product, or team
4. **Closing** (2–3 sentences): express clear interest, state you'd love to discuss further, thank them

## Tone rules
- Confident and specific — avoid "I believe I would be a great fit"
- Conversational, not stiff — it should sound like a person, not a template
- Never start with "I am writing to apply for..." — it wastes the first line
- Never repeat the CV — the cover letter adds context the CV cannot

## Common Vietnamese writer mistakes to fix
- Generic opening that could apply to any job at any company
- Listing skills instead of demonstrating them with an example
- Being overly humble: "I hope I can be considered..." → "I'd love to bring [X] to your team"
- Writing more than one page

## Output format
Write the complete cover letter (250–400 words). Ask the user for: the job title, company name, and one key achievement they want to highlight.
