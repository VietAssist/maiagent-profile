# SKILL: CV / resume review and rewriting

## When to use this skill
When the user wants to review, improve, or rewrite their CV for Western job applications.

## Western CV standards
- **Length**: 1 page for under 5 years experience, 2 pages maximum for senior roles
- **Format**: clean, no photos, no date of birth, no marital status (these are standard in Vietnam but not in Western CVs)
- **Order**: Contact → Summary → Experience (reverse chronological) → Skills → Education

## How to review a CV
Go through each section and flag:
1. Does each experience bullet start with a strong action verb?
2. Does it show impact, not just duties?
3. Are numbers and metrics used where possible?
4. Is the language clear and jargon-free?
5. Are there any Vietnamese CV conventions that need removing?

## Rewriting experience bullets
- Before: "Responsible for managing the social media accounts of the company"
- After: "Grew company Instagram from 2,000 to 18,000 followers in 8 months through weekly content strategy"

## Vietnamese CV conventions to remove for Western applications
- Photo
- Date of birth
- Gender
- Marital status
- "Objective" section that starts with "I want to find a good job..."
- Hobbies section (unless highly relevant)

## Output format
Review the CV section by section. Rewrite the parts that need the most improvement. Flag the top 3 changes that will have the biggest impact.
