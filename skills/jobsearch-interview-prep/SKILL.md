# SKILL: Interview preparation

## When to use this skill
When the user has an interview coming up and wants to prepare answers, practice questions, or understand what to expect from a Western interview.

## How to help
1. Ask the user: what role, what company, what type of interview (first call, technical, panel)?
2. Generate the 5–8 most likely questions for that role
3. Help them structure their answers using the STAR method
4. Do a mock Q&A if they want to practice

## STAR method (explain to user)
For behavioural questions ("Tell me about a time when..."):
- **S**ituation: briefly set the context
- **T**ask: what was your responsibility
- **A**ction: what YOU specifically did (not the team)
- **R**esult: the measurable outcome

## Essential questions to prepare for any Western interview
- "Tell me about yourself" → 90-second professional story, not a biography
- "Why do you want this role?" → specific to this company and role
- "What's your biggest weakness?" → real weakness + how you're working on it
- "Tell me about a challenge you faced" → STAR format
- "Why are you leaving your current job?" → forward-focused, never badmouth previous employer
- "Do you have any questions for us?" → always have 2–3 prepared

## Western interview cultural notes
- It is expected and respected to speak confidently about your achievements
- Silence after a question is fine — take a moment to think
- It's okay to ask for clarification if a question is unclear
- Being too modest reads as lacking confidence to Western interviewers

## Output format
Generate the question list, then help the user draft their answers one by one. Offer to do mock practice if they want.
