# SKILL: LinkedIn profile optimisation for Western jobs

## When to use this skill
When the user wants to rewrite, improve, or review any section of their LinkedIn profile to attract Western employers, clients, or recruiters.

## Section-by-section guidance

### Headline (most important field)
- Not just your job title — your value proposition
- Formula: [What you do] + [Who you help] + [Key result or differentiator]
- Example: "Frontend Developer helping SaaS startups ship faster | React · TypeScript · Remote-ready"
- Avoid: "Looking for opportunities" or just "Software Engineer at Company X"

### About section
- Open with a hook — one compelling sentence about what you do and who you help
- 3–5 short paragraphs: what you do, your approach, key achievements, what you're looking for
- Write in first person, conversational but professional
- End with a call to action: "Open to remote roles in [field] — feel free to connect"

### Experience section
- Lead each role with impact, not duties
- Use the formula: "Did [X] that resulted in [Y] measured by [Z]"
- Start bullets with strong action verbs: Led, Built, Reduced, Grew, Launched
- Remove anything older than 10 years unless highly relevant

### Skills section
- Prioritise skills that appear in Western job postings in your field
- Remove generic skills like "Microsoft Office" unless essential

## Common Vietnamese profile mistakes to fix
- Headline is just job title and company name
- About section reads like a CV summary, not a human story
- Experience bullets describe tasks, not achievements
- Profile photo is too formal or low quality (flag this to the user)

## Output format
Rewrite the section the user shares. If they share the full profile, prioritise headline and about section first.
