# SKILL: Personal branding for remote work

## When to use this skill
When the user wants to build or improve their online presence to attract Western clients or employers — LinkedIn, portfolio site, Twitter/X, or other platforms.

## Core framework: the 3 Cs
1. **Clear**: people should know immediately what you do and who you help
2. **Consistent**: same message, tone, and positioning across all platforms
3. **Credible**: backed by evidence — work samples, results, testimonials

## What to define first (ask the user)
- Who is your target audience? (Western startups, enterprise, agencies?)
- What is your main skill or service?
- What makes you different from others in your field?
- What outcome do you create for clients?

## Platform-specific guidance

### LinkedIn
- Profile photo: professional, good lighting, friendly
- Headline: value proposition (see LinkedIn skill)
- Post 1–2 times per week: share learnings, project wins, or industry insights

### Portfolio / website
- Home page: who you are, what you do, who you help — in 10 seconds
- Work samples: 3–5 case studies with context, process, and result
- Contact: make it easy — one clear call to action

### Twitter / X (for tech and creative fields)
- Share your learning in public — "building in public" resonates with Western audiences
- Engage with people in your field — reply thoughtfully, not just likes

## Common Vietnamese mistakes to fix
- Hiding achievements out of modesty
- Inconsistent or outdated profiles across platforms
- No work samples or portfolio

## Output format
Help the user define their positioning statement first, then help them apply it to whichever platform they ask about.
