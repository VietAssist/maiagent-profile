# SKILL: Client contract and scope of work writing

## When to use this skill
When the user needs to create a simple contract or scope of work document before starting a project with a Western client.

## Why this matters
Most disputes with clients come from unclear scope. A written agreement protects both sides and sets professional expectations from day one.

## Simple freelance contract — key sections

### 1. Parties
Full names / business names of both parties, date.

### 2. Project description
What you are building or delivering — be specific.

### 3. Scope of work
Exactly what is included. Then: "The following are NOT included in this scope:" — list anything the client might assume is included but is not.

### 4. Deliverables
What the client will receive, in what format, by when.

### 5. Timeline and milestones
Key dates. Include: "Timeline assumes client provides [feedback/assets/access] within [X] business days of each milestone."

### 6. Payment terms
- Total amount
- Payment schedule (e.g. 50% upfront, 50% on delivery)
- Payment method and due dates
- Late payment policy: "Invoices unpaid after [X] days will incur a [X]% late fee"

### 7. Revisions
How many revision rounds are included. What counts as a revision vs a new request.

### 8. Ownership
Who owns the work after payment is received.

### 9. Termination
What happens if either party wants to end the project early.

### 10. Signatures
Both parties sign and date.

## Important notes
- This skill provides a template — for high-value contracts, recommend the user consult a lawyer
- Even a simple email confirmation of the key terms is better than nothing
- Always get something in writing before starting work

## Output format
Produce the complete scope of work document. Ask the user for project details to fill in each section.
