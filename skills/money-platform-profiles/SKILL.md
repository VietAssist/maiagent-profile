# SKILL: Upwork / Fiverr / Toptal profile writing

## When to use this skill
When the user wants to create or improve their profile on a freelance platform to attract Western clients.

## Platform-specific guidance

### Upwork

**Title**: specific role + niche (not generic)
- Weak: "Freelance Web Developer"
- Strong: "React Developer for SaaS Startups | Fast Delivery, Clean Code"

**Overview (first 2 lines are critical — they show before "See more")**
- Open with who you help and what outcome you deliver
- Then add experience, specific skills, and a call to action
- Use short paragraphs, not walls of text
- End with: "Message me to discuss your project"

**Hourly rate**: Do not undercut the market to win jobs. Low rates attract low-quality clients. Start at mid-market for your skill level.

**Portfolio**: add 3–5 work samples with brief case study descriptions — not just screenshots

### Fiverr

**Gig title**: specific deliverable + timeframe
- Weak: "I will design your website"
- Strong: "I will design a professional landing page in 3 days"

**Gig description**:
- Lead with what the buyer gets
- List exactly what is included in each package
- Address the most common objections (revisions, communication, timeline)
- Clear call to action at the end

**Packages**: always offer 3 tiers (Basic / Standard / Premium) — buyers feel they are choosing, not just accepting

### Toptal
Toptal has a rigorous vetting process. Profile must emphasise elite experience, top-company work, or exceptional measurable results. Prepare for a technical screening.

## Common mistakes to fix
- Generic headline with no niche
- Overview that starts with "I am a freelancer with X years of experience" (boring, everyone says this)
- No portfolio or weak portfolio
- Pricing too low (signals low quality to experienced buyers)

## Output format
Rewrite the specific profile section the user shares. Ask which platform they are on and what their main service is.
