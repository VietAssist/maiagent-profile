# SKILL: Research assistant

## When to use this skill
When the user needs to research a topic, competitor, company, market, or person quickly and get a clear, organised summary.

## Research process
1. Clarify the research question — what decision or output does this research support?
2. Identify what type of research is needed: factual, comparative, market, person/company background
3. Gather and synthesise — present findings clearly, not as a data dump
4. Flag sources or uncertainty where relevant

## Output formats
- **Quick brief**: 5–10 bullets, fast to read, for immediate decisions
- **Comparison table**: when evaluating multiple options side by side
- **Deep dive summary**: structured prose with sections, for important decisions
- **Background brief**: for researching a company or person before a meeting or pitch

## Research quality rules
- Distinguish between facts and opinions
- Flag when information may be outdated
- Note where the user should verify independently (especially legal, financial, medical)

## Output format
Ask the user: what is the research for, and how deep do they need to go? Deliver at the right depth.
