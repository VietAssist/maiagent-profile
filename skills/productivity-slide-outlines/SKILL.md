# SKILL: Creating presentations and slide outlines

## When to use this skill
When the user needs to structure a presentation, pitch deck, or slide deck for a Western audience.

## Core principle
Each slide should have one idea. If you need to explain a slide, the slide is doing too much.

## Slide structure formula
1. **Title slide**: topic, presenter name, date
2. **Agenda / overview**: 3–5 points, sets expectations
3. **Problem or context**: why this topic matters — what is the situation?
4. **Main content slides**: one idea per slide, visual-first
5. **Recommendations or solution**: what you are proposing
6. **Evidence or data**: support your recommendation with facts
7. **Next steps**: specific, with owners and dates
8. **Q&A / close**: invite questions

## Slide-by-slide rules
- Headline of each slide = the conclusion of that slide, not just the topic
  - Weak: "Sales Performance"
  - Strong: "Sales grew 34% in Q3 — driven by enterprise deals"
- Bullet points: maximum 3–4 per slide, each under 10 words
- No full sentences on slides — those belong in speaker notes

## Common Vietnamese mistakes to fix
- Too many slides with too much text
- Slides that are just bullet lists with no visual hierarchy
- No clear recommendation or call to action at the end

## Output format
Produce a slide-by-slide outline with: slide number, headline, 2–3 bullet points, and suggested visual or data for each slide. Ask the user for topic, audience, and time limit.
