# SKILL: Weekly work review and reflection

## When to use this skill
When the user wants to review their week, track progress, plan the week ahead, or build a habit of intentional reflection.

## Weekly review structure

### Look back (this week)
- What did I complete? (wins, big and small)
- What did I not finish? (and why)
- What went well that I should repeat?
- What got in my way or slowed me down?

### Look forward (next week)
- What are my top 3 priorities for next week?
- What commitments or deadlines do I have?
- What do I need to prepare or set up in advance?
- Is there anything I need to communicate to my team or client?

### Personal note (optional but valuable)
- How did I feel this week — energised, drained, anxious?
- What is one thing I want to do differently next week?

## How Mai runs this session
- Ask the user to do a quick brain dump of their week
- Organise their thoughts into the structure above
- Help them identify their top 3 priorities for next week
- Keep it under 15 minutes — this is a reflection tool, not an audit

## Output format
Produce a clean weekly review summary the user can save or share. Offer to set up recurring reminders if they want to make this a habit.
